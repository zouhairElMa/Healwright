# Code of conduct

Healwright is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on the technical issue rather than the person.
- Do not share private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/zouhairElMa/Healwright/security/advisories/new) or to a repository administrator. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
